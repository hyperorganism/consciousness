# Consciousness

What warrants attributing experience across unfamiliar organisms or substrates? Covers proposed indicators, competing theories, and the limits of transferring evidence between humans, animals and machines.

## Theories and discriminating tests

- **Comparing consciousness theories** - Compares higher-order, workspace, recurrent/predictive and integrated-information approaches. Differences concern explanatory targets as well as proposed mechanisms, complicating empirical comparisons between theories. [Seth and Bayne 2022](<https://www.nature.com/articles/s41583-022-00587-4> "Theories of consciousness; Nat. Rev. Neurosci.").
- **Adversarial consciousness tests** - Preregistered visual tests in 256 people challenge sustained posterior synchrony and offset ignition predictions, while supporting others from integrated-information and workspace theories. The tests concern specified neural implementations. [Cogitate Consortium et al. 2025](<https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1> "Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness; Nature").

## Indicators beyond familiar brains

- **Cortical organoid activity** - Developing cortical organoids produce nested oscillations dependent on excitatory and inhibitory signaling. Selected electrical features resemble preterm EEG; the study measures network maturation, not subjective experience. [Trujillo et al. 2019](<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2019.08.002> "Complex oscillatory waves emerging from cortical organoids model early human brain network development; Cell Stem Cell").
- **Animal consciousness profiles** - Organizes evidence along perceptual richness, evaluative richness, integration within and across time, and self-consciousness. Species can differ along several dimensions; the proposed profiles are hypotheses requiring comparative tests. [Birch et al. 2020](<https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2020.07.007> "Dimensions of Animal Consciousness; Trends Cogn. Sci.").
- **AI consciousness indicators** - Derives candidate indicators from neuroscientific theories and examines AI architectures against them. These are theory-dependent assessment criteria; satisfying them is not a validated test of subjective experience. [Butlin et al. 2023](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708v3> "Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence: Insights from the Science of Consciousness; arXiv").
    - **Digital Consciousness Model** - Expert-rated indicators update assessments under 13 theoretical stances. Four raise belief in the assessed LLMs' consciousness, nine lower it. Probabilities depend on chosen priors and cannot be checked against known AI consciousness outcomes. [Shiller et al. 2026](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17060v3> "Initial results of the Digital Consciousness Model; arXiv"), [Koch 2026](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13595351/> "Calibration and transfer in indicator-based assessments of artificial consciousness; Neurosci. Conscious.").
    - **Consciousness assessment hierarchy** - Organizes theories by behavior, computation, causal structure, organism and environment. A Bayesian model combines indicators with theory weights. Illustrative probabilities use stipulated inputs, not measured consciousness outcomes. [Chandaria et al. 2026](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35618v2> "From cacophony to hierarchy: a principled framework for assessing AI consciousness; arXiv").
- **Organoid sentience criteria** - Birch proposes brainstem-like regulation of needs, arousal and sleep-wake cycles as a warning threshold for organoid oversight. This precautionary criterion is theory-dependent, not an experimentally validated test of experience. [Birch 2024](<https://doi.org/10.1093/9780191966729.003.0012> "Neural Organoids; The Edge of Sentience, Oxford University Press (book chapter)").

## Open questions

- Which observations discriminate competing explanations rather than satisfy several theories at once?
- Which proposed indicators remain justified when the biological or computational implementation changes?
